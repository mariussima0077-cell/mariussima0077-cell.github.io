Torque Empire – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Torque Empire ("the game") is an idle garage game. This policy explains what data the game uses. Short version: we do not collect your name, e-mail, contacts, location or any other personal data, and the game shows no ads.

1. Data stored on your device

Your game progress (money, buildings, mechanics, gear, campaign progress, settings) is saved only in your phone's storage. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

2. Anti-cheat check (anonymous)

To keep the game fair, the game creates a random anonymous player ID (for example 3f9c…) and, when you are online, sends a small summary of your progress to our anti-cheat server every few minutes: money, cars sold, reputation, VIP points, building levels, campaign stage, number of tickets and heroes, the game version and technical warning signs (for example "the device clock was moved back"). The server compares these numbers with what is possible in the game. The data is not linked to your name or Google account, is not sold or shared, and is deleted after 180 days without activity. The server runs on Cloudflare (see Cloudflare's privacy policy).

3. Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. To confirm a purchase, our server sends the purchase token to Google's Play Developer API and stores the token with your anonymous player ID, so the purchase can be restored and refunds or fraud can be detected.

4. Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

5. Your choices

You can play offline; then nothing is sent. To ask for deletion of the anonymous anti-cheat data, write to us with the Player ID shown in the Shop.
