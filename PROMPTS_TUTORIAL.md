## 1. MAMA ROSA – personajul-ghid (5 poze)

```
Create a sprite sheet for a mobile idle-tycoon game called TORQUE EMPIRE.
STYLE: high-quality 2D mobile game art like AFK Arena / Rush Royale: bold clean outlines, rich cel shading, glossy highlights, warm saturated colours, slightly chibi proportions (big head, expressive face), polished premium look. NOT pixel art, NOT blocky, NOT photorealistic.
BACKGROUND: solid flat pure green #00FF00 everywhere, no shadows on the background, no gradients, no floor.
CHARACTER: "Mama Rosa", the friendly chief mechanic who teaches new players. Woman in her 40s, warm confident smile, curly dark-brown hair in a big bun under a yellow-and-gold bandana, blue racing goggles with gold rims on her forehead, small gold hoop earrings, rolled-up pale pink work shirt, dark brown leather overalls with gold buckles and a gold "+" emblem on the chest, big golden oil-can / grease-gun tool on her back, tool belt with wrenches.
LAYOUT: 1 row of 5 separate poses, same size, same scale, HALF BODY (from the waist up), each pose clearly separated with green space between them, nothing touching or overlapping:
1. TALKING – waving hello with one hand, mouth open, happy.
2. POINTING – pointing with her index finger to the RIGHT side, explaining.
3. THUMBS UP – proud thumbs up, wink.
4. THINKING – hand on chin, one eyebrow raised.
5. CELEBRATING – both arms up, big laugh, a few small gold sparkles around her (sparkles stay inside her own cell).
Image size 1536x1024. No text, no letters, no frames, no UI.
```
