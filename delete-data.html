Torque Empire – Delete your data

Torque Empire (by Torque Studios) does not collect your name, e-mail or location. Our server stores an anonymous player ID with a summary of your game progress (used to stop cheating) and – only if you chose to sign in with Google – your cloud save linked to a one-way code of your Google account ID.

Delete your cloud save / Google link (in the game, instantly)

Open Torque Empire → Shop → box ☁️ Cloud save → DELETE (tap twice). The cloud save and the link to your Google account are removed from our server at once. The game on your phone stays.

How to request deletion by e-mail

  1. Open Torque Empire and go to the Shop. Note the Player ID shown at the bottom.
  2. Send an e-mail to torqueempirehelp@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and your Player ID.
  3. We delete all data linked to that Player ID from our server within 30 days and confirm by e-mail.

What is deleted

The anonymous player ID and all progress reports linked to it (money, cars sold, reputation, VIP points, building levels, campaign stage, tickets, heroes, game version and anti-cheat warning signs). If you signed in with Google, the cloud save and the Google link are deleted too. Nothing is kept.

If you do nothing, the anti-cheat data is deleted automatically after 180 days without activity and a cloud save after 2 years without use. Your game save on your phone is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.
