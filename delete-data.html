Torque Empire – Delete your data

Torque Empire (by Torque Studios) has no accounts and does not collect your name, e-mail or location. The only data stored on our server is an anonymous player ID with a summary of your game progress, used to stop cheating.

How to request deletion

  1. Open Torque Empire and go to the Shop. Note the Player ID shown at the bottom.
  2. Send an e-mail to torqueempirehelp@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and your Player ID.
  3. We delete all data linked to that Player ID from our server within 30 days and confirm by e-mail.

What is deleted

The anonymous player ID and all progress reports linked to it (money, cars sold, reputation, VIP points, building levels, campaign stage, tickets, heroes, game version and anti-cheat warning signs). Nothing is kept.

If you do nothing, this data is deleted automatically after 180 days without activity. Your game save on your phone is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.
